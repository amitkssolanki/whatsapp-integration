# Participant guide

Thank you for helping. This is a test of a WhatsApp ordering system for a pretend
restaurant, "The Local Table". **Nothing is charged and no food will arrive.** You are
helping us check that the system does what it should when real people use it.

## How to start

1. Open <WA_ME_LINK> (or save <DEMO_NUMBER> and message it).
2. Send "Hi".
3. Tap the catalog button in the reply and browse the menu.
4. Add a few items to the cart and send the cart.

## What you will get back

- A welcome message with a catalog card.
- An order receipt right after you send your cart.
- Later (minutes to a few hours, a person is looking at it), a message saying the
  order was accepted or rejected. That message may not come at all on some days. That is
  fine and worth telling us.

## What we may ask on specific days

Amit messages you beforehand. Typically one of:

- Place an order.
- Reply "Hi" to a message.
- Stay silent for a day, then place an order.
- Order one specific item that we name.

Please do only what was asked that day. Between sessions, you do not need to do anything.

## Please do not

- Put real personal details in messages beyond normal chatting. No payment information, ever.
- Share the number or link publicly.
- Send many messages quickly. Space them out; one order, then wait for the reply.
- Forward or screenshot the chat to anyone but Amit.

## Privacy

Your WhatsApp number and display name are stored on a private server and deleted
after the test period, on the purge date (<PURGE_DATE>). Only counts and statuses remain.
Any screenshot shown publicly has numbers and names masked.

## If anything looks odd

Message Amit directly with the time, what you saw, and a screenshot if you can. A
missing reply, a late reply, or a strange message is useful, not a mistake on your side.
You can stop at any time by telling Amit.

## How long

About 3 weeks, 2 to 4 short sessions (a few minutes each).
